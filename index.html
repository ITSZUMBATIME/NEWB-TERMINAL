NEWB
A terminal for people who are new to the terminal.

Getting started

Drag a command from the Cheat sheet into the terminal. NEWB types it for you and waits. You press Enter when you're happy with it.

Blue blanks are for you to fill in. Just type. Tab jumps to the next blank.

Drag a file or folder from Files into the terminal to type its name, or onto a blank to fill it.

Search in plain English in the cheat sheet, like "delete a file" or "wifi".

Ctrl+C copies when text is selected, and stops a running command when it isn't. Ctrl+V pastes.

Make everything bigger or smaller with Ctrl++ and Ctrl+-. The Display button has more.
